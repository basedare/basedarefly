# Proposed discovery experiments A–E

All five are PROPOSED, NOT RUN and NOT MEASURED. No interviews, partner agreement, media permission, outreach, traffic allocation or live release is implied. Reuse current routes, attribution, permissions and commercial gates. The already shipped QR/harness rail is the baseline, not work to rebuild.

Use [the experiment record](template.md). Begin with a few separately consented observed tasks in the existing Siargao journey; these uncover friction, not population effects. An A/B comparison needs a reviewed unit/assignment, sufficiently powered plan, stable inventory and comparable follow-up. The current six-field QR export alone cannot provide most outcomes below. Do not add tracking merely to fill a template.

| Experiment | One hypothesis/change | Evidence and outcome to seek | Main risks / stop and rollback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Positioning | Clear outcome-led entry copy may help a traveller explain the offer and choose an eligible real activity. Compare one wording change with current copy; keep the brand and destination. | First use a neutral comprehension task and unassisted first-action observation. Later compare qualified starts per landing and separately reviewed completions after equal follow-up, not registrations. | Misunderstood rewards/verification, hidden effort or reduced completion quality stops the test. Restore recorded copy. |
| B — Discovery friction | Asking a simple intent and showing one grounded next action may reduce effort for an anonymous visitor. | Observe an actual nearby activity search → details → existing start. Record task success, taps, median time/range, empty/error cases and help required. Compare the same destination, inventory, intent and device/network. | Wrong city/time/budget, fabricated availability, blocked guest progress, inaccessible control or moderation leak stops the test. Revert the small control/ranking change. |
| C — QR acquisition | A specific current local destination may yield more useful starts than a generic entry. Use only the existing permissioned, current-source QR rail. | Compare qualified browser-journey/target landings → starts by matched placement/channel/window, with real-phone render failures recorded. Verify later participation separately. Scans are a delivery diagnostic. | Host permission/revocation failure, source mismatch or misleading reward/presence claim stops the test. Pause the affected link or restore the prior canonical destination; never erase attribution. |
| D — Authentic proof | A genuine, currently approved and permissioned story may clarify what an activity involves. | Observe comprehension and first-action tasks with/without one relevant story, same activity/effort. Later inspect starts and appropriate reviewed completions with equal follow-up. An image view is not proof of trust or a visit. | Missing consent, withdrawal not honored, misleading official endorsement, complaint or extra moderation burden stops the test. Remove the presentation; preserve withdrawal/tombstone history. |
| E — Creator recruitment | A specific legitimate opportunity with clear scope/effort/status may be more understandable than generic recruitment. | First test comprehension of existing opt-in application screens. Later, only after separate contact authorization, compare eligible applications, accepted deliverables and support burden against matched opportunity/cohort/window. | No eligible/funded inventory means no paid invitation. Unwanted contact, false income promise, private roster leak or unsuitable applications stops the test. Restore copy and stop the authorized campaign; retain opt-outs. |

For each attempt predeclare completion-quality, complaint, moderation-burden and reliability limits. Treat unknown guardrails as unknown. Any consent/privacy, authorization or paid-launch violation is an immediate stop. Qualitative evidence can justify a small correction but cannot establish lift, product-market fit or a universal psychology rule. Record selection, novelty, seasonality, repeat browsers, bot and attribution limitations, including inconclusive or negative results. Founder approval remains required for release; this backlog does not authorize messages or production changes.
