# Bounded experiment record

Status: PROPOSED / APPROVED / RUNNING / STOPPED / COMPLETE. Default: PROPOSED. An entry does not launch an experiment or authorize contact.

- ID, owner and decision date:
- Existing journey and one change:
- Observed problem, source and confidence/uncertainty:
- Hypothesis and alternative explanations:
- Baseline revision, candidate revision/PR and rollback revision:
- User impact, estimated complexity and safety risks:
- Evidence provenance, unit, qualification/exclusions and deduplication:
- Primary outcome/denominator and follow-up interval:
- Comparison method, source/channel, placement, destination/inventory, device/network, timezone/weekday and window duration:
- Assignment/contamination and attribution limitations (or why this is a qualitative task):
- Required tests and actual receipts, separately:
- Independent reviewer and founder release decision:
- Consent/media permissions and host permission, where applicable:
- Guardrail baseline and predeclared stop limits: completion quality, complaints/withdrawal, moderation time/queue and reliability:
- Planned sample/stopping rule, chosen before results; no significance claim from a convenient stopping point:
- Observed outcome with sample sizes and uncertainty: NOT MEASURED until collected.
- Guardrail outcomes and collection gaps: UNKNOWN until collected.
- Decision: retain / reject / revise / unknown; rationale and remaining unknowns:
- Rollback action and verification, if taken:

Keep sanitized summaries only. No raw identities, transcripts, private media, exact locations, secrets or payment records. Preserve prior/negative decisions and distinguish measured facts from hypotheses. Report median and spread for time/effort; do not substitute an outlier-sensitive mean without context. No guest-browser metric establishes distinct people or presence.
