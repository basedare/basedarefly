# SiargaoVibes competitive read

## Evidence

As observed on 2026-07-27:

- SiargaoVibes packages island discovery into restaurants, tourism, nightlife, surf, activities, events, NGOs, services, and artists.
- Its venue pages emphasize hours, directions, bookmarks, reviews, business claims, and sponsored visibility.
- Its physical Wakepark banner uses a tracked campaign URL. This validates physical QR distribution where travellers are already deciding what to do.
- Its published Wakepark Sunday Funday occurrences were dated March–April 2026. The founder separately field-confirmed on 2026-07-27 that Sunday Funday still runs with DJs and a free wakeboard ride with a drink purchase. The field confirmation, not the old event page, is the current source and expires unless reconfirmed.
- Siargao Wakepark publicly describes an action-sports lake in Barangay Consuelo with wakeboarding, wakeskating, hydrofoil practice, a water zipline, ice bath, minigolf, and basketball.

## What this validates

Siargao has proven demand for:

- One local answer to “what should I do?”
- Predictable weekly rituals.
- Physical QR acquisition at high-intent venues.
- Activity categories beyond food and nightlife.
- Commercial visibility for local businesses.

SiargaoVibes' current advantage is coverage, local SEO, and physical distribution. BaseDare should not answer by becoming a second static directory.

## BaseDare's differentiated move

Convert local information into a verified action loop:

`discover → choose intent → navigate or save → participate → verify → update place memory → return a receipt`

The durable assets are freshness, evidence, contributor reputation, and receipts—not listing volume.

## What to borrow

### 1. Fresh local rituals

Add recurring rituals only as a freshness-aware schedule layer:

- Each ritual needs a source or owner, recurrence rule, next occurrence, last confirmation, and expiry/recheck state.
- An occurrence may open directions, a Mission Pass, or an “is this still happening?” field question.
- A ritual becomes a paid bounty only when reward escrow is actually funded.
- Never infer that an old event page is currently live.

This extends the existing Tonight layer and Outcome Contract rail. It is not a second event platform.

### 2. Action-sports participation

Use Wakepark, skate, surf, fitness, and wellness places as playable discovery supply:

- Community Sparks can be free, clearly labeled, safety-bounded challenges with no earnings promise.
- Paid missions must be funded and independently verified.
- Venue permission is required before representing an official competition, schedule, or perk.

### 3. Physical QR distribution

Use the existing Field Station OS:

- A static Wakepark banner or table QR is an acquisition node.
- It can carry station, creative, and intent attribution.
- It cannot prove presence, authorize a reward, or act as the secure venue handshake.
- Secure rotating QR plus accepted evidence remains the higher-trust transition.

### 4. Civic and impact missions

Package cleanups, animal welfare, libraries, and community work as permissioned Mission Kits:

- Partner or land-manager approval.
- Explicit safety and disposal instructions.
- Evidence matched to the real outcome.
- No spontaneous volunteer labor presented as official NGO work.

### 5. Media support

A photographer or filmer can become an opt-in production service, not an automatic verifier:

- Scoped fee and availability.
- Separate evidence reviewer.
- Explicit commercial-reuse consent before sponsor reuse.
- Build only after action-sports mission density creates repeat demand.

## What not to copy

- Generic star ratings and passive bookmarks as the primary product.
- Pay-to-verify or pay-to-trust “gold” pins.
- Another broad filter taxonomy competing with the intent question.
- Unfunded pots, stale weekly schedules, or estimated attendance presented as live.
- A creator marketplace before there is repeat mission demand.

Promoted placement may exist only when labeled **Promoted**, frequency-capped, and kept separate from proof strength and place trust.

## Build order

1. Add Siargao Wakepark as an ordinary discovery place.
2. Pilot a permissioned Field Station QR there or at another willing activity venue.
3. Seed a small number of safe Community Sparks after the map has delivered value.
4. Run the freshness-aware Wakepark ritual as publicly reported founder-confirmed information; seek venue-maintained confirmation before representing it as official.
5. Add filmer routing only after repeated action-sports missions reveal a real buyer and participant need.

## Success measures

- Useful action opens per station scan.
- Directions or Mission Pass saves per useful answer.
- Verified arrivals per directions open.
- Accepted place-memory updates per ritual.
- Repeat participation and repeat venue funding.

Directory page views remain interest, not physical outcomes.
