# Real-world retention, not screen-time retention

## Locked loop

**Curiosity → commitment → belonging → visible impact → repeat crew.**

BaseDare should retain people by making a useful real-world plan easier to join and repeat. It should not optimize for infinite browsing, fake scarcity, randomized rewards, streak anxiety, or time spent in the app.

## Product language

- Discover: **What is happening here right now?**
- Evaluate: place · time · people · ability or useful constraint · honest cost
- Join: **3 confirmed · 1 more makes it**
- Commit: **You’re in** · map · calendar · invite
- Unlock: **Crew unlocked** only when real participants cross the stated minimum
- Return: **Plan ended. Go again?**

RSVP is intent, not attendance. Without a secure check-in or accepted proof, BaseDare may say a plan filled or ended; it may not say people showed up or completed it.

## What already exists

- Live Plans aggregates boats, meetups, events, Sparks, and paid Dares.
- Rally and boat cards show real participant counts and minimums.
- Plan Links explain the real activity before signup.
- My Next Move preserves joined plans.
- Rally repeat links preserve activity, place, and crew threshold.

## Highest-value reinforcement

The missing seam is immediately after joining. Turn a lightweight RSVP into a concrete when-and-where plan:

1. acknowledge the commitment
2. keep the plan persistently reachable
3. offer map, calendar, and invite actions
4. signal the real threshold crossing once
5. offer a fresh repeat only after the plan ends

## Sensory rule

Haptics belong only on meaningful transitions: a soft selection pulse when joining and a stronger success pulse when a real crew minimum is crossed. No constant sound, flashing, or motion.

## Metrics

North star: **weekly real-world plans completed by two or more people**, once completion can be honestly observed.

Near-term software metrics:

- plan opened → RSVP
- RSVP → calendar add
- RSVP → invite shared
- invite opened → RSVP
- real crew thresholds crossed
- ended RSVP’d plan → repeat Rally started

Do not report RSVP or calendar adds as verified attendance.
