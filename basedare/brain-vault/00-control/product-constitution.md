# BaseDare Product Constitution

**BaseDare is the social network for the real world. THE WORLD IS YOUR FEED. Belong. Explore. Dare.**

The core is one living Feed + Map connecting people, canonical places, authentic content, experiences and actions. People may explore alone, meet others, share, try free Dares or pursue genuinely funded work. Venues, creators, hosts and operators create value together. Siargao is Node001; prove usefulness and repeat participation there before expansion. This dated founder decision supersedes earlier market-tournament and proof-first product positioning; those historical records stay intact.

Real-world discovery, meaningful participation, communities and Dares distinguish the product. Following is not friendship or chat membership. A personal story is not a verified visit, paid proof or official venue announcement. A reviewed display is not an endorsement, licence, crowd or commercial outcome. Preserve the original PeeBear, approved wordmark and restrained gold/purple tactile design.

Initial commercial validation is a legitimate operator referral and a separately contracted promotional service. No unapproved price, partner, commission, sale or successful payment may be invented. Financial canon remains authoritative: applicable V2 bounty settlement96/4/0, no Live Pot entitlement; Verified Field Sprint is a separate service/reward rail. Tips, if later approved, are voluntary direct external support with0% BaseDare fee; no custody or bounty-fee assumption.

Build now: connect existing discovery, a narrow reviewed ordinary-text publishing journey, canonical place actions, first-minute/mobile usability, Dares/sharing and honest measurement. Reuse Local Signals/FounderEvent, existing identities, moderation, venues, community, plans, Moments and attribution. No new feed database or parallel dashboard.

Build after evidence: public follows/saves and a meaningful Following view, actual media/device acceptance, one consenting musician's support pilot and a real host's Session. Commercial validation needs a real operator, consented offer, fulfilled booking and reconciled commission—not a speculative booking marketplace. Defer autonomous payouts/custody, token launches, auctions, global Chapters, AI ranking and new social infrastructure.

Before a major feature, answer: which existing journey improves; what already exists; what can be reused; how participation/retention/revenue improves; how it can be simpler; whether it creates a disconnected screen; how success is measured; what can wait; whether it preserves this constitution. A significant departure needs a dated explicit founder decision.

Core metric: weekly known identities completing meaningful actions, with guest browser records, self-reports, independently verified evidence and actual cash separated. Views/clicks are not presence or sales. Preserve privacy, consent, withdrawal, owner authorization, RLS, moderation, historical records, payment safeguards and human mainnet gates. No production fixtures, messages or financial commitments. The November P0 ends at a reviewed PR; founder approval precedes merge/deployment.
