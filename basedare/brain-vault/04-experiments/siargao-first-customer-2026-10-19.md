# First Siargao buyer and participant cell

Hypothesis: a short product walkthrough with a relevant local business and an opt-in creator can identify one useful, budget-owned brief and willing participants before paid amplification.

Six prospects researched; four email drafts prepared; zero sends recorded in this batch. No replies, meetings, onboarded participants, signed scopes, revenue or paid outcomes are claimed. A business listed on the map is not a partner.

Queue: [[../02-outreach/drafts/siargao-first-customer-queue-2026-10-19.csv]]. Exact draft messages: [[../02-outreach/drafts/siargao-first-customer-batch-2026-10-19]]. Plan: [first revenue operating plan](../../docs/gtm/first-revenue-operating-plan-2026-10-19.md).

Proposed first cell: 10 discovery testers, six screened field contributors (four assigned plus two reserves only after funding), one creator/manager, one buyer with a decision. These are targets, not allocated budget or recruited people.

Primary metric: actual qualified buyer conversations → scope → funded, delivered receipt → full-price repeat decision. Secondary: opt-in participant attempts, unaided completion, support cost and voluntary return. Views, drafts, wallet connections and reward volume are not customer revenue.

Start condition: sender/channel and first-batch scope confirmed; connected sending account's history/opt-outs checked. Paid stages additionally require guarded human-signed mainnet cutover, real payout/refund rehearsal, budget and legal/support/review owners. Physical device and paid proof checks remain mandatory.

Stop on: decline/opt-out, duplicate contact, wrong-account data, lost proof, duplicate transfer, unsupported rights, inability to fulfil, or a paid-readiness question we cannot answer accurately. No automatic follow-up is approved. The result remains pending until observed, not when a draft is finished.
