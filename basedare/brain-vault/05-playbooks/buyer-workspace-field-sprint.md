# Buyer Workspace + Verified Field Sprint

## Locked hierarchy

- **Control Mode** is BaseDare's buyer-facing operating layer.
- **Verified Field Sprint** is the first managed commercial offer.
- **Buyer Workspace** is where a buyer scopes and tracks commissioned work.
- **Creator Radar** is routing infrastructure. It is not an equal product on the buyer entry screen.
- **First Spark** remains a venue-specific template for authorized partners. It is not the default buyer wedge.

## First-time buyer promise

> Get verified answers from the real world. Tell BaseDare what you need to know and where. We route independent contributors, verify the evidence, and return timestamped results with a receipt.

Lead with the deliverable:

- one bounded question;
- one place or micro-area;
- four independent checks;
- seven-to-ten-day turnaround;
- supporting evidence and field notes;
- accepted, rejected, and inconclusive decisions;
- one timestamped receipt.

Do not lead with the service/reward split. Show the fixed quote during scoping, before the invoice request. The canonical economics remain unchanged.

## Buyer journey

1. **Question** — what must BaseDare answer?
2. **Coverage** — which place or micro-area?
3. **Evidence** — what must each answer show?
4. **Budget** — show the fixed quote and deliverables.
5. **Review** — request the invoice and confirm the scope.
6. **Routing** — BaseDare assigns contributors after payment clears.
7. **Receipt** — buyer receives the evidence set, decisions, payout status, place memory, and timestamped receipt.

The buyer describes the outcome. BaseDare solves routing. A recommended-contributor shortlist may appear later as an optional advanced override, after the question and geography are known.

## Public entry rules

- One dominant action: **Scope a Field Sprint**.
- Secondary proof: **See an example receipt**.
- Returning-user escape: **Open your Buyer Workspace**.
- Never present Field Sprint, Buyer Workspace, and Creator Radar as three equal products.
- Never promise traffic, sales lift, a positive answer, or automatic approval.
- Never imply that a venue is a partner unless it has explicitly opted in.

## Operating metric

Track:

- scope started;
- question + coverage completed;
- invoice requested;
- invoice confirmed;
- contributors assigned;
- accepted/rejected/inconclusive answers;
- receipt opened;
- repeat Sprint requested.

The commercial north star is **paid Sprints that return a complete, auditable receipt and create useful place memory**.
