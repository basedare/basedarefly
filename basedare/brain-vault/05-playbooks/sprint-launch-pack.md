# Sprint Launch Pack

Updated: 2026-08-04

## Core positioning

The old line is too agency-coded:

> Give us one local question. We send verified people into the field and return proof.

Use this instead:

> Turn real-world curiosity into playable proof.

Supporting line:

> BaseDare turns the question into playable missions, routes contributors, verifies what happened, and attaches the receipt to the place.

BaseDare has three faces:

- Consumer front: a playable social map for travellers and locals.
- Contributor engine: a performance-verified dare marketplace for creators and players.
- Buyer asset: a place-memory receipt engine for venues, brands, tourism operators and sponsors.

The core loop is:

`fund question → player action → verified proof → place memory → receipt → repeat`

Do not collapse BaseDare into only a social media app, only an influencer network, only a fieldwork agency, or only a check-in app. The map is the feed; verified actions are the content; receipts are the durable asset.

## Packet 1 — Creator Starter Pack

Goal: give each creator one clear thing to film, one link to share, and one proof angle.

Checklist:

1. Pick one creator and one concrete local hook.
2. Choose the exact BaseDare action their viewers should open.
3. Create a tracked Creator Drop link.
4. Give the creator one short caption, one short script and one proof prompt.
5. Tell them views are useful, but verified actions are what BaseDare measures.

Creator DM:

> I want you to narrate one real Siargao action, not run an ad. You post the story, viewers open your BaseDare link, and we measure who actually starts or completes the action.

## Packet 2 — Sprint Operator Checklist

Goal: turn one buyer question into four independent checks and one conservative receipt.

Checklist:

1. Name one design partner and the exact question they care about.
2. Choose the place or small area being tested.
3. Choose two permissioned Field Station hosts only if they improve acquisition.
4. Fund the creator pool before routing contributors.
5. Route four independent contributors; never let one person answer the whole sprint.
6. Review proof, record limitations, pay accepted contributors and close with a receipt.

Buyer one-liner:

> Give us one local question. BaseDare turns it into four verified checks and returns a receipt you can actually make a decision from.

Good questions:

- Is this venue visibly active between 7–9pm on Tuesday?
- Which two cafés are actually laptop-friendly today?
- Is this event still happening this week?

## Packet 3 — Venue / Field Station Packet

Goal: give a venue or station host one clear reason to place a QR and one truthful result afterwards.

Checklist:

1. Get permission before placing any QR or table card.
2. Use one promise only: tonight, mystery, social, reward, or free roam.
3. Check local inventory before activating the QR; never route a Tonight scan to an empty map.
4. Keep poster QR acquisition separate from secure venue QR/check-in proof.
5. Report station-host interest separately from destination-venue verified outcomes.

Host pitch:

> This QR helps guests answer “what should I do next?” We will show you scans and useful actions. We will not call scans foot traffic.

Physical card:

> New here? PeeBear can fix your next two hours. Scan for the live island map. No download needed.

## Packet 4 — Receipt Close

Goal: end every experiment with what happened, what did not happen, and the next repeat ask.

Checklist:

1. Separate verified arrivals, accepted proof, paid contributors and station scans.
2. Include negative and inconclusive answers instead of hiding them.
3. Show rights and limitations clearly; do not imply purchase or guaranteed traffic without evidence.
4. Ask one repeat question, not a broad “do you want more?”
5. Record the result in place memory when it is approved and useful.

Receipt close:

> Here is what BaseDare verified, what we could not verify, what it cost to learn it, and the cleanest next question to fund.

Repeat ask:

> Want us to run the same proof window again next week, or test a different question while the signal is fresh?
