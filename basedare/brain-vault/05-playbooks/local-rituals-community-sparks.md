# Local Rituals, Community Sparks and Action-Sports Supply

## The product distinction

BaseDare must never collapse these four objects:

| Object | What it means | Money claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local Ritual | A recurring, sourced local schedule with a next occurrence and freshness deadline | None unless a separate live escrow is attached |
| Community Spark | A self-directed, safety-bounded free challenge | Explicitly no cash payout |
| Funded Dare | A real mission with live escrow and the existing proof/review/settlement rail | Only the escrowed amount |
| Impact Mission Kit | Permissioned, bounded sponsor/community work with authorization and outcome evidence | Only after authorization and real funding |

A schedule is not proof that an event is currently running. A QR scan is not proof of arrival. A free challenge is not a competition, venue endorsement, or earnings opportunity.

## Fresh local rituals

Every recurring ritual stores:

- venue and recurrence;
- local timezone and start/end window;
- source label and optional source URL;
- last confirmation and automatic freshness expiry;
- permission state;
- optional funded Dare reference.

`CONFIRMED` means the schedule has not passed its recheck deadline. `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` means BaseDare must stop presenting it as current until a human reconfirms it. `PAUSED` removes it without deleting history.

Wakepark Sunday Funday is the first canonical ritual: Sunday 12pm–6pm, DJs, and a free wakeboard ride with a drink purchase, recorded from founder field confirmation on 2026-07-27. Conditions can change; the venue remains authoritative.

## Community Sparks

Community Sparks exist to make the map playable before paid mission density exists. They must:

- be safe for a participant operating at their existing ability;
- use public or customer-authorized space;
- contain instructions and a visible safety boundary;
- state `Free Community Spark · no cash payout`;
- avoid claiming to be an official contest, class, venue offer, or endorsement;
- never create simulated liquidity that looks like funded work.

The alpha registry covers Wakepark, Malinao skate, Marco and Kanaway surf schools, Surfit, and PrimeFit. An operator may activate only the presets that remain accurate and appropriate on the ground.

## Wakepark Field Station

A Wakepark Field Station is a permissioned acquisition node:

`physical QR → ACTION answer → ritual or Community Spark → venue/directions → optional Mission Pass → verified action`

Activation requires recorded placement permission. The static QR can preserve station and creative attribution, but it cannot check someone in, prove presence, unlock a perk, or authorize money. Existing destination GPS plus rotating secure venue QR or accepted mission proof remains the high-trust boundary.

The ACTION lane falls back to the neutral nearby answer when fewer than the configured number of fresh, safe ritual/Spark options exist.

## Permissioned impact missions

Cleanup and animal-welfare Mission Kits require recorded partner authorization before Sprint compilation or funding. The immutable contract snapshot retains that authorization fact.

The brief must name:

- consenting organizer/venue/land authority;
- exact zone, window, task, and supervisor;
- safety equipment and stop conditions;
- disposal, welfare, or service handoff;
- privacy boundaries;
- evidence supporting the real outcome.

No spontaneous volunteer activity may be presented as official NGO or venue work.

## Verified Filmers — parked boundary

Filmers may later become opt-in production support with a stated fee and availability. They are not automatic truth authorities:

- a filmer cannot judge or approve their own evidence;
- availability and fee must be explicit;
- participant and location safety remains primary;
- sponsor commercial reuse remains blocked until the explicit, versioned consent rail is built and human legal review is complete.

Do not build a filmer marketplace until repeated action-sports missions reveal real paid demand.

## Operating gate

Release the software and run one permissioned Wakepark station only after:

1. the production migration succeeds;
2. placement permission is recorded;
3. Sunday Funday is reconfirmed or allowed to become stale automatically;
4. at least the configured minimum ACTION inventory exists;
5. one real-device QR → answer → destination handoff works;
6. the station receipt reports scans as interest and only secure arrivals/completions as physical outcomes.

The orchestration wrapper stays parked until one Verified Field Sprint and one manual Creator Drop have completed through the existing human-operated rails.
