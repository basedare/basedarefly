# Manual Creator Drop Pilot

## Purpose

Run one Creator Drop with the Dares system that already exists. The goal is not volume. It is to learn whether one human operator can turn one useful buyer brief into one safely completed, correctly paid, reusable real-world result without hidden manual gaps.

## One-Drop contract

Before funding, write:

- one named buyer or internal design-partner owner;
- one precise real-world question or bounded action;
- one place, time window, and location radius;
- one eligible creator profile;
- required evidence and explicit rejection reasons;
- live gross reward and who funded it;
- the receipt that should exist if the attempt clears.

Use an approved Outcome Contract/Mission Kit. A truthful supported `NO` or `INCONCLUSIVE` answer is valid for Field Truth. Do not choose a desired answer.

## Human run sequence

1. **Preflight:** verify coordinates, time window, radius, trusted upload path, Sentinel access, payout wallet, and sufficient real escrow.
2. **Route:** invite one qualified creator or publish through the moderated open-claim lane.
3. **Acknowledge:** confirm the creator understands Go / Do / Prove / Win / Earn and the safety stop conditions.
4. **Observe:** let the existing claim, upload, GPS evidence, append-only attempt, review, appeal, lease, and settlement rails operate.
5. **Review:** accept or reject evidence on the stated contract only. Do not rescue a weak result because the pilot needs a success.
6. **Settle:** prove exactly one payout or an authoritative non-payout state.
7. **Receipt:** inspect actor, result, evidence limitations, place-memory effect, fees, payout, and timestamps.
8. **Debrief:** record operator minutes, creator friction, review cost, failure points, and whether anyone asks to repeat.

## Required adversarial attempt

The first operating batch must include one deliberately rejectable attempt in a safe test context—for example stale, incomplete, or clearly outside the evidence requirement—so rejection and recovery are exercised without encouraging unsafe conduct.

## Stop rules

Stop rather than improvise if:

- escrow or production migration is missing;
- the creator or venue lacks required authorization;
- coordinates/radius are wrong;
- media rights are required but not granted;
- safety conditions change;
- proof cannot be durably recorded;
- payout ownership/state is ambiguous.

## Success gate for orchestration

Do not build the automatic orchestration wrapper until:

- one complete Verified Field Sprint has four independent settled results and its buyer receipt;
- one manual Creator Drop has a canonical claim → proof → review → settlement → receipt trail;
- at least one negative/inconclusive Field Truth result was handled truthfully;
- one first attempt was rejected and recovered correctly;
- operator costs and manual decisions are recorded.

Only then automate repeated, boring decisions. Never automate authority, proof approval, real-money spend, or sponsor rights merely because the happy path worked once.
