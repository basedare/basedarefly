# Acquisition engine — tracked participation and human follow-up

First reviewed increment of the shared Acquisition & Retention Funnel brief. Reuses CreatorAttributionLink, AttributionJourney/Touch/Event, ActionIntent, CreatorDropAssignment and the existing business intake. No migration, dependency, paid completion, public Moment activation or external message.

## Changes

- Existing `/admin/creator-drops` gains a focused Chain Reaction kit: choose an actual free adventure, source channel, real owned/consenting account and unique link code. Operator-attested channel/placement permission is mandatory. Existing authorized attribution POST derives the exact challenge target from the catalogue; `/go` opens that challenge, never a generic signup wall. A prepared URL is not a published post or a venue partnership.
- Existing invitation queue accepts these links alongside Creator Drops. Free outreach copy is voluntary, with no cash/reach/feature promise. Link metrics now scope intents and Mission Passes to the link’s own primary touch, fixing shared-target overcounting. Free play is never judged by a paid verified-completion result.
- After a recorded self-reported finish, guests can optionally prepare a fresh-attempt invitation. Opaque per-intent/run links deduplicate, have a durable per-journey daily cap, and reuse the existing `/go` rail. Only allowlisted public browsing context transfers, rounded to three decimals; private notes, sender run/progress, identity and results do not. Unavailable tracking falls back to ordinary sharing.
- Native/copy actions are separately client-reported. Cancellation creates no share-action record, and nothing claims delivery. An independent recipient gets their own journey/progress. No paid intent is completed or reward/rights/presence inferred.
- Existing founder Moment growth panel gains capped channel/challenge cohorts. Redirect entries and client-reported rendered landings are distinct. Starts, self-reported finishes, invite actions, distinct recipient-browser starts and currently approved contributions are shown with missing-stage/identity limitations. Overflow returns unavailable instead of partial totals or guessed conversion rates.
- Existing business operator queue adds a human follow-up owner and separate conversation/proposal stage. These are operator records, not automatic messages, purchases, accepted contracts or verified sales. Existing notes, next action, paid-state guards and financial evidence remain unchanged.

## Validation

The actual disposable PostgreSQL/handler journey covers source link → recorded render/start/finish → invitation prepare/copy action → independent recipient start, exact source separation, owner refusal, private-context removal, retry dedupe, existing creator queue, human follow-up persistence without a campaign, and bounded cohort overflow. Guest/browser fixtures are not real people or delivered invitations. Existing migration/privacy and discovery checks remain required.

See [the acquisition/retention audit](../launch/acquisition-retention-audit-2026-11-02.md) for genuine readiness and the first-week operating plan. Retention email is the next separate reviewed increment. Provider/domain/inbox evidence remains required before collecting promotional subscribers. Both Moment flags, originals, paid settlement, performance rewards, bulk email and Telegram broadcasts stay unchanged.
