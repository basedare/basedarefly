# Discovery, contribution pilot and business atmosphere

This increment builds the safe P0 of the two Discovery/Product Intelligence briefs and the Rarelist contribution brief. The founder explicitly requested implementation and pushing to main. Baseline main is b432cdbf (PR47 QR/harness); final CI, merge and live receipts are recorded after release, not inferred from this document.

## What changed

- Existing Map/Pulse **Ask PeeBear** gains one “What are you up for?” form and quick intents. Existing picks remain behind More PeeBear picks; the map stays at the top on phones. No conversation history, new AI provider, external model cost or implicit geocoder is added.
- The reusable deterministic pipeline interprets intent, now/next2h/tonight/tomorrow, stated PHP budget, duration and company preference. It retrieves bounded canonical records, rechecks existing public/consent/block/revocation policies, applies eligibility before ranking and gives explicit actions/reasons. Unknown prices/durations cannot satisfy a stated constraint. Comma/decimal budgets and destination calendar/DST boundaries have regressions. Future outdoor plans still pass daylight checks at their actual start.
- Published events/meetups, free community challenges, authored self-guided adventures, reviewed Moments and community stories retain their distinct labels. Past stories are not live events. Paid intent uses the existing recorded-funding/open-mission/rights catalogue plus fresh payment readiness; browse coordinates grant no participation, presence or money rights. The global paid catalogue cap24 and per-source80/read-result12 bounds remain selections, never exhaustive absence/count claims.
- Optional **Explore connections** disclosures connect existing place, creator, Moment, story and current activity sources on Map/Pulse cards and their existing detail pages. The read-only projection follows canonical references, explicit creator credit and current ownership, rechecking after retrieval. A transferred tag or withdrawn credit cannot retain an old creator edge. No private wallets, media CIDs or device location history is returned.
- Universal header search now also excludes assigned/unfunded/expired or ineligible Dares, revoked/blocked creators and unavailable attached places. Current canonical inactive/blocked places cannot be resurrected by the curated fallback. Creator copy uses public handles rather than wallet fragments. Search failures are generic and private/no-store.
- The existing offline harness now adds confidence, uncertainty, impact, complexity, safety, required tests, guardrails and rollback to hypotheses. Existing six aggregate fields and missing-versus-zero rules remain. Interview questions, modest experiments A–E, primary-source competitive findings and measurement definitions are documented; no interviews, lift, unique humans or product-market fit are invented.
- The original ParticleNetwork atmosphere is carried onto the actual business entry, with static nodes for constrained/reduced-motion devices. The homepage switch runs MatrixRain before routing to the same three-goal business page. A bounded fallback prevents decorative loading from stranding navigation; timers cancel on exit and reduced motion skips the transition. The old business homepage is not restored.
- **Add pins & updates** is now visible within the expandable map tools. The former Add place hint was written into private-spot-only state and could be invisible; it now opens the actual guidance. Existing public photo/location/signing/review and private device Save spot paths remain distinct. FAQ explains both.

## Contribution pilot: recognition, not new financial infrastructure

Existing FounderEvent stores immutable versioned recognition policies, private drafts and append-only operator audit. Existing PointsEvent stores an `xp:` namespace for non-cash awards and compensating reversals. Creator Passport explicitly excludes this namespace from Signal Points. No migration, dependency, token, cash balance, fee, payment, customer, sponsor or payout is created.

The existing creator-drops admin receives a small draft/review panel. It supports policy windows/activity IDs/optional canonical venue, bounded XP/caps, activation/pause/terminal close, source-based awards, reversal and campaign-wide leaderboard consent withdrawal. Actor, wallet and XP are server-authoritative. Global transaction locking, evidence deduplication, one visit/place and creation/activity, lifetime caps and terminal reversals prevent retries/reward farming. Draft retries are idempotent.

Supported sources are deliberately narrow: an existing confirmed VENUE_QR / QR_AND_GPS visit with session/token/distance evidence, or an approved permissioned AdventureSubmission with actual review and source. A creation review never asserts attendance. Page views, personal steps, likes, followers and referral opens do not earn pilot XP. Existing referral attribution stays separate; automated referral awards, Dare-completion XP, sponsor rewards and business revenue integration remain deferred.

An active, enabled pilot has an Island Trials seasonal permalink, genuine dates/rules/eligible adventure actions and consented public standings. Current source/identity/withdrawal, explicit opt-in and audit reversal govern public scores; overflow is unavailable rather than partial/zero. Public fields contain no wallet or private evidence. Operator opt-out preserves XP/history while removing public standings. Self-service opt-out is not implemented; establish a named support/manual withdrawal route before activation.

## Configuration, privacy and boundaries

- `DISCOVERY_INTELLIGENCE_ENABLED=false` is an optional kill switch; default is the approved deterministic feature. Legacy picks/map/adventures remain available when paused.
- `CONTRIBUTION_PILOT_ENABLED` must equal `true` for activation, awards or public reading. **Production remains off.** Draft administration, pause/close, reversals and consent withdrawal remain available while disabled. No live pilot is published by this release.
- Existing Moment social/original intake, media providers, email and paid-launch gates remain unchanged. No new credentials, environment mutation, migration, hosted fixture, external message, money movement or campaign promise is included.
- Discovery uses the selected map area, rounded public browsing context and a25km maximum. It does not silently move to a city named in prose. Unsupported dates/currencies are disclosed. Venue hours remain unconfirmed. Location permissions are optional; choosing a city is not presence evidence.
- New free text is POSTed for the current request, not persisted or sent to analytics. Only structured intent/window/kind/count/surface/duration bucket go through existing optional PostHog capture. Client query/impression/open instrumentation is not verified participation or proof of analytics ingestion. Existing canonical start/review/completion/share/referral handlers retain their independent evidence.

## Graphify and future integration

Graphify continues to index actual code and is rebuilt after this increment. The harness guardian now requires discovery, relationship and contribution sources. Runtime connections are a bounded projection over existing Prisma foreign keys and consented records, so source create/update/unpublish/report/withdrawal changes are seen on subsequent reads without a second graph database or stale ETL. Graphify itself does not store travellers or certify participation.

A future approved ChatGPT Apps/MCP integration can expose read-only `search_published_experiences` and `get_public_connections` over these same validated services: bounded area/query/time, canonical public IDs, private/no-store and rate limits, source timestamps/reasons and existing BaseDare participation links. No tool may publish, sign in for another person, unlock presence, collect private history, claim money or change review policy. Deployment/auth/account scopes require a separate reviewed integration. No ChatGPT app or MCP server is published in this sprint.

## Verification and outstanding acceptance

Parser policy checks, actual disposable PostgreSQL/handler journeys and harness checks cover budgets, timezone/DST, current sources, credit/ownership races, blocks, withdrawal, source limits, safe paid selection, guest reads, origin/body/rate controls, non-financial XP idempotency/reversal/opt-out and unchanged Signal Points. Main/source CI, typecheck, lint, optimized build and browser verification are required before ship. Do not substitute fixtures for physical phones, genuine creator consent, provider upload/playback, email delivery, a fulfilled commercial sale or mainnet payout/refund.

Public pilot activation needs a real organizer/support owner, genuine contribution/phone/provider acceptance and actual participant/leaderboard permission. Keep campaigns draft and the pilot flag off until those checks and a specific pilot approval. Original P2 commercial validation and later adaptive personalization remain open.
