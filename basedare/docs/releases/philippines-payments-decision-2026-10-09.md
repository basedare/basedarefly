# BaseDare: pay like a local in the Philippines

Research checked 9 October 2026. Decision: build the BaseDare experience around a user-controlled USDC wallet; partner for conversion, merchant settlement and bank/e-wallet payouts. Do not launch a new stored-value currency or require Bare coin to pay for a meal.

This is a product and engineering assessment, not a legal opinion. Philippine counsel and the actual contracting providers must confirm BaseDare's permitted role before embedded live payments. A brand's technical coverage does not establish its licence or permission for our business model.

## What changes the decision

Travellers should be able to discover a place, earn a reward and spend it without an ATM trip. That is a useful product loop. However, local QR payment alone is already competitive: Coins.ph supports stablecoin-funded QR Ph checkout, RedotPay supports Philippine QR payments, and GCash now offers GTourist. BaseDare's advantage should be the connection to activities, people and participating venues, with clear payment receipts.

**Two different products require separate approval:**

- **Buying a meal:** customer approves a specific merchant QR payment; provider converts crypto and pays the merchant in PHP.
- **Paying a creator or venue:** an approved payout service sends PHP to the named beneficiary's GCash/bank account. Merchant checkout support does not automatically permit contractor disbursements, arbitrary transfers or third-party beneficiaries.

## What “build our own” would actually mean

Building our own screen is reasonable. Running currency conversion, holding customer balances, maintaining PHP liquidity, settling merchants, handling fraud/disputes and offering withdrawals is a different business.

BSP Circular 1108 covers specified virtual-asset services. The BSP's M-2025-031 continued the new-VASP-licence moratorium from September 2025; the current Citizen's Charter still references it. Do not plan launch around quickly obtaining a fresh VASP licence. [VASP FAQ](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Media_and_Research/Primers%20Faqs/FAQs_VASP.pdf), [moratorium](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/2025/M-2025-031.pdf), [Citizen's Charter](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/CitizensCharter/bspcitizenscharter.pdf).

Other relevant regimes are separate: EMI rules for qualifying stored monetary value; OPS registration/payment-system rules; merchant acquisition requirements where applicable; and SEC CASP rules for relevant crypto-asset activities. The EMI closed-loop definition refers to acceptance only by the merchant-issuer and its subsidiaries/affiliates. Calling a balance “venue credit” does not automatically exempt a network of unrelated venues. Counsel must assess the actual issuer, redemption rights, custody and funds flow. [Circular 1166](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Published%20Issuances/Images/Circular_1166.pdf), [Circular 1198 FAQ](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/2024/1198%20-%20FAQ.pdf), [SEC CASP rules](https://www.sec.gov.ph/mc-2025/sec-mc-no-04-series-of-2025the-sec-rules-on-crypto-asset-service-providers-sec-casp-rules/).

Partnering reduces the systems we operate; it does not automatically exempt BaseDare from applicable obligations. Non-custodial branding alone is not a legal determination. Identity, sanctions, transaction monitoring, consumer complaints and privacy responsibilities need a written allocation. Keep identity documents with the provider wherever possible.

## Technical fit with the current build

The code currently connects existing EVM wallets through Wagmi, including a Coinbase Smart Wallet option. It does not give every signed-in user a newly provisioned wallet. Mission Pass email recovery restores saved activities, **not wallet keys or money**.

Use native USDC on Base as the initial asset, with one wallet per user where supported. Display available on-chain USDC separately from rewards awaiting approval/settlement, funds committed to dare escrow, and venue-specific credit. Never count a pending payout as spendable. A fiat top-up should be a provider-hosted purchase delivered directly to the user's wallet; receiving USDC from another wallet is a different, already feasible path.

Yodl's SDK reference explicitly demonstrates Base USDC and EIP-1193 wallet providers, so the asset/network fit is promising. Its documented packaged UI is React Native. A hosted WebView being used internally does not prove supported embedding in BaseDare's Next.js website; do not improvise an iframe wallet-signing bridge. [SDK API](https://docs.yodl.me/sdk/react-native/api).

Yodl notifications use signed, versioned payment snapshots. Its integration-address documentation warns that another app can declare our integration address. Therefore webhook authenticity alone cannot prove a BaseDare campaign purchase. A future adapter must match a server-created payment intent, authenticated user wallet, provider reference/transaction, merchant identifier, amount, asset, chain and quote expiry. Dedupe by provider/chain/transaction and ignore older versions. Reconcile missed events through the Payments API. Keep crypto transfer, provider success, merchant receipt, and refund status distinct until the provider explains precisely what each state guarantees. [Notifications](https://docs.yodl.me/integrations/notifications), [attribution limitations](https://docs.yodl.me/integrations/integration-address), [Payments API](https://docs.yodl.me/integrations/payments-api).

The existing LocalSpendPilot is deliberately an external Yodl app link, shown only after an owner-recorded successful merchant-QR test. Its purchase evidence is manual. Clicking the link does not move the connected BaseDare balance into Yodl, complete a payment, verify a visit or award a perk. Keep that distinction visible until an actual shared-wallet integration passes testing.

## BaseCash decision and economics

Keep existing BaseCash obligations and records intact. Do not convert balances into USDC or a new coin. Current BaseCash is venue-specific credit with fixed PHP denominations, a 24-hour validity window, manual payment/settlement handling and no cash-out. Its code charges PHP40/75/175/300 on PHP500/1,000/2,500/5,000 credit: 8%/7.5%/7%/6% of face value, before any funding FX cost. It also has a configurable rate defaulting to 56 PHP/USDC. This is not a live FX quote or a general payment wallet.

For ordinary meals, provider checkout avoids adding a credit-purchase and staff-redemption step. Keep BaseCash, if useful, for explicitly venue-funded offers with clear redemption terms. Do not charge its existing service fee on top of a Yodl payment. Do not present current credit as interchangeable with wallet money.

Yodl SDK pricing describes processor fees/minimums, FX, gas, a currently waived 0.10% protocol fee and optional wallet convenience fees with a 30% share to Yodl. Treat these as published terms to confirm commercially. PH documentation currently lists one live processor: aggregation does not yet imply local redundancy. AEON publishes PH non-KYC limits of USD200 per transaction, USD500 daily and USD5,000 monthly; KYC limits differ. These are provider claims, not BaseDare's approved limits or a basis to bypass verification. [Yodl pricing](https://docs.yodl.me/pricing), [countries](https://docs.yodl.me/supported-countries), [AEON limits](https://aeon-xyz.readme.io/docs/transaction-limits).

Compare total stablecoins debited for the same PHP merchant receipt, including failed-payment/refund costs. Coins' advertised collection pricing does not equal a quote for spending at someone else's merchant QR. Coins documents PHP refunds for its crypto-funded QR checkout; RedotPay describes returns to USDT and potentially delayed confirmation. Refund currency and timing belong in checkout/support, not a generic “instant” promise. [Coins refund description](https://www.coins.ph/en-ph/blog/pay-with-peso-crypto-or-both-coins-ph-pioneers-stablecoin-payment-utility-in-the-philippines-with-first-of-its-kind-qrph-integration).

## Integration sequence and acceptance criteria

1. **Now:** compact wallet balance/receive section in My activity; keep USDC, ETH gas and venue credit intelligible. Existing external Yodl pilot stays explicitly separate. No new token or balance ledger.
2. **Partner diligence:** seek Yodl/AEON checkout and Coins/PDAX payout terms. Obtain legal entity/licence mapping, foreign-passport eligibility, Base USDC support, supported QR variants, actual web integration, quote expiry, settlement guarantees, beneficiary rules, refunds, support SLA, reconciliation and commercial pricing. No outreach is sent without authorization.
3. **Sandbox integration:** implement the chosen provider's real API, not a speculative abstraction. Show recipient, PHP received, exact USDC debit, all fees and expiry before signature. Persist payment intents/idempotency keys; signed notifications plus reconciliation; no browser-only success crediting. Separate refund/support queue from dare escrow.
4. **Limited field pilot:** proposed two willing venues, ten participants, twenty purchases, with provider/operator approval. Test mobile Brave/Safari, slow data, rejected signing, double taps, lost callbacks, expired quotes, unsupported merchant/personal QR, wrong network, insufficient gas, denied KYC, delayed settlement and refund. Confirm actual merchant receipt every time; zero unexplained balance differences and no duplicate charge before expanding.
5. **Then:** provider-hosted card/bank top-ups and approved GCash/bank beneficiary payouts. Explicitly prove both meal checkout and payout flows; do not infer one from the other.

Bare coin can be evaluated later as an optional loyalty/community feature after retention is demonstrated and its legal/economic design reviewed. Meals, rewards and refunds should work without buying a volatile platform token. No token issuance, sale, redemption promise or speculative price incentives are built in this pass.

## Still required before paid launch

Mainnet escrow is prepared, not deployed. Complete human-signed deployment, runtime checks and a tiny funding/payout/refund trial. Email recovery code is fixed but still needs a verified sender and real inbox trial. Venue staff must complete one real QR + fresh GPS check-in trial. Payment-provider onboarding and embedded settlement are not completed by this research or by the wallet UI.

## Implementation and verification

Implemented a collapsible wallet section in My activity, reached from the account menu's Your wallet link. It reads canonical USDC and ETH directly from the configured chain, separates unavailable/loading/zero balances, and provides a receive address only on the correct supported network. Unsupported testnet provider configurations and demo mode cannot expose a real receive control. Reads occur when expanded; no continuous polling or new transaction signing was added. This does not automatically create a wallet or supply a fiat top-up provider.

BaseCash request creation now verifies the connected wallet using the existing authenticated-session/action-signature helper. An arbitrary typed wallet address is no longer accepted. A mocked route-boundary test proves unauthorized requests produce neither credit rows nor alerts, and checks the verified-wallet throttle. These IP/wallet limits are per process; they are not globally enforced across serverless workers. Existing manual payment confirmation, credit liabilities and settlement rules are unchanged and still need a real field trial.

Verification: application typecheck passed; targeted lint has zero errors and one pre-existing dashboard image warning. Desktop and 390px mobile browser checks covered wallet expansion, actual read-only balance lookup using a public test address, receive disclosure and no horizontal overflow. No wallet signing, real transfer, real credit request, partner activation or email was performed. Production database compatibility passed on retry: 59 migrations and 77 models. Graphify rebuilt. The optimized application build passed with local/preview gate mode after the separate production database check passed; existing middleware/dependency/OnchainKit configuration warnings remain. The normal main-branch Vercel deployment retains its production database gate.

Rollback: revert the wallet panel/menu and BaseCash client/server changes together if the wallet fails to load or verified customers cannot request credit. Do not weaken authentication as a quick fix; keep credit requests unavailable until repaired. There is no migration to reverse. Existing issued credits are preserved.
