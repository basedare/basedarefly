# Rarelist lessons and controlled recognition

Reviewed November11,2026. [Rarelist’s public projects page](https://rarelist.io/projects) describes campaign discovery, X posts/tasks, RARS standings and campaign rewards; [its home](https://rarelist.io/) describes creator contribution and earned access. These are its published mechanics, not proof of verified customers, growth, reward delivery or a suitable BaseDare business model.

Useful transfer: one understandable campaign, explicit contribution rules, current review status, visible progress, voluntary invitations and clear eligibility. BaseDare applies those to actual place/activity evidence. A private step completion, social impression or invitation click is not a verified real-world action.

| Graphify | Current code index gains the new sources and guardian checks. Runtime links reuse existing canonical records; no runtime Graphify entity store exists to synchronize. |

P0 is an operator-managed **non-cash recognition pilot**, default-off, not the full universal contribution/reputation/rewards economy. No real campaign, participant, sponsor, score or reward pool is seeded in production. Campaigns are born private drafts. Do not advertise XP as money or future opportunity entitlement.

## Before enabling one real pilot

Name the organizer/moderator/support owner and provide a functioning request route for leaderboard opt-out, appeals and source corrections. Obtain genuine participant/display/leaderboard permission; sponsor reuse remains separate. Choose real dates, activity/place scope and bounded immutable XP rules. Test the real contribution/review/withdrawal on both phones and any needed media provider. Inspect a draft and approve that specific pilot. Enabling code is not that approval.

Operator consent withdrawal hides the participant campaign-wide without deleting XP/history; current source withdrawal, revocation or invalidation removes public eligibility. Corrections use audited compensating reversals. Self-service opt-out and automatic withdrawal reconciliation of historical ledger totals are later work; raw historical ledger sums are not a current eligible/public score.

No financial promises, speculative token/airdrop, automatic social detection, follower gate, mandatory social credentials, secret account scraping or self-service funded pool is copied from the inspiration. Business/revenue outcomes still require actual fulfilment and reconciliation. Recognition is not proof of demand.

Measure a small consenting Siargao cohort’s useful participation, review burden, invalidations, complaints, voluntary recipient participation and return. The suggested10–20 cohort is a test target, not current usage. Separate reviewed creation from independently verified real-world participation and unique identities from humans. Publish an inconclusive result when evidence is small; the existing harness cannot prove causal lift from a higher headline count.
