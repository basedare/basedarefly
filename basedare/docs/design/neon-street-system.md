## Logo direction — plan only

The current graffiti wordmark stays in production for this pass. A credible identity system needs a simpler master as well as an expressive illustration.

1. Refine the existing letterforms, spacing, braces and silhouette as editable vector artwork. Preserve recognition; simplify tiny chips and interior highlights that disappear at header size.
2. Explore a small cream X beneath the BASE/DARE junction, with a dark outline and one restrained cyan or magenta offset. Use PeeBear’s rounded crossed strokes. Treat it as a signature, not a red error/cancel mark. Keep it within the existing logo footprint so header spacing stays stable.
3. Compare the clean wordmark and X lockup at 96, 112 and 224px, in monochrome, on light/dark surfaces, in print and as social crops. The full lockup should remain recognizable without glow.
4. Build a separate compact bear-eye X / braces symbol for app icons rather than shrinking the whole wordmark. Keep the detailed bear as the campaign mascot.
5. Present two or three concrete variants before choosing the new master. Ship SVG and transparent raster exports with clear-space and minimum-size guidance after the choice.

Recommendation: use the X as an optional signature lockup, with the refined clean wordmark as the durable company mark. Scale comes from recognition, legibility and repeatable execution, not extra effects.
