# BaseDare personal Battles — format for validation

The current shipped foundation is free play, reviewed Moments, a permissioned weekly editorial pick and a **Two takes** page for an actual approved parent/reply pair. A comparison is public-display discovery, without scores or winners. It is not a named two-person competition. Keep ordinary exploration and going together available; rivalry is optional.

Before introducing a named Battle, validate this small format with two real willing creators:

1. **Invitation and consent:** one creator proposes a single existing challenge and their own approved Moment. The other accepts using their own authenticated identity and their own approved independent attempt. Neither a public URL, an invitation click nor existing display/Spotlight permission substitutes for specific Battle consent. No automatic pairing or inferred friendship.
2. **Bounded scope:** freeze the two source IDs, exact prompt, declared start/end and destination timezone. Start only after both consents. A proposed pilot lasts seven days; that is a design proposal, not an active deadline. No participant count, opponent or time pressure is displayed before a real accepted pairing exists.
3. **Eligibility and safety:** human review of permission, public/private details and suitability before publication. Originality, wit and an imaginative take on the prompt are the proposed criteria. Danger, harassment, humiliation, unconsented filming, drinking pressure, emergency imitation and purchased engagement cannot improve a result. Follow existing age/account rules; do not introduce a public prize category without its own approved eligibility rules.
4. **Judging:** start with a comparison and no result. Any later editorial judging requires a named accountable reviewer, published criteria and a documented conflict/recusal process before the start. A Spotlight is an editor's pick, never a retrospective competition win.
5. **Duration and withdrawal:** show actual dates only after acceptance. Either creator may withdraw; an account change does not transfer consent. Reporting, rejection, deleted/private social sources and revoked permissions suspend the pairing and its card. Preserve records privately under the existing retention policy; never delete a contribution just to make a result work.
6. **Vote integrity:** public voting is not implemented. Before any voting experiment, define identity/duplicate controls, self-voting and conflict rules, rate limits, suspicious-vote review, attack handling and appeals. Wallets are not unique humans. Outside social likes, views and followers never determine a BaseDare winner by themselves.
7. **Disputes:** freeze a disputed result, retain the source/reason/reviewer audit and offer the existing support/report route. Set an explicit response owner and review window before running a judged programme. Do not automatically penalize, publish accusations or trigger payment from a report.
8. **Privacy and rights:** share only the approved creation and consented credit. No precise location, contact information, private notes or wallet address in the public comparison/card. Display, credit, editorial selection, Battle participation, promotional contact and commercial reuse remain separate choices. Existing consent cannot silently become an advertising license.
9. **Rewards:** no cash, points, pay-per-view or automatic payouts in this format. A future perk must be explicitly authorized by the venue, genuinely available and governed by its existing allocation/redemption rules. A cash commission/prize needs real agreed terms, eligibility, funding, review, dispute/refund rules and the normal paid-launch readiness checks before it can be advertised. Reward pools are not platform revenue; preserve the financial canon.
10. **Smallest later implementation:** reuse AdventureSubmission IDs and the existing moderation/FounderEvent audit, with explicit independently signed consent records for both participants. Define atomic acceptance, cancellation, replay protection and read-time consent checks before exposing a named Battle. Do not build a new wallet, competition currency, voting ledger or media feed as a prerequisite.

Measure actual challenge opens where configured, self-reported starts/finishes, Moment referrals, authenticated replies, approved reply records, repeat journeys and reviewer time. Distinguish events, browsers, wallets and people; views, invitation delivery and causal sales need separate evidence. Test whether a recipient voluntarily makes a real independent attempt before investing in voting or monetary competitions.
