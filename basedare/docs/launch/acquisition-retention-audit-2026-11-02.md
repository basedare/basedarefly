## First-week measures

- By exact tracked source/challenge: redirect-entry journeys, separately client-reported rendered landings, start journeys, self-reported finish journeys, share-action journeys, independently identified recipient-browser starts and currently approved contribution records.
- Keep missing stages visible; do not manufacture conversion percentages when client events were not recorded. Frozen attribution may attach a later action to an earlier touch.
- Separately: actual creator invitations/acceptances/postings, saved business enquiries, operator-recorded buyer replies/proposals and independently verified paid outcomes from their existing financial evidence.
- At day seven, review where the sequence stops: no starts → improve the hook/destination; starts without finishes → improve the activity; finishes without invitation actions → improve the personal invitation; enquiries without replies → improve human follow-up. These are diagnostic hypotheses, not causal proof.

## Remaining acceptance

Do not activate paused submissions, originals, paid settlement, performance rewards, bulk email or Telegram broadcasts. A provider's accepted send is not inbox delivery. Email requires a controlled sender/domain with verified DNS, securely configured provider credentials, a founder-owned inbox acceptance, consent/unsubscribe/suppression checks and explicit rollout. Real-device/installed-app and physical venue QR checks need the founder; fixtures are not substitutes.
