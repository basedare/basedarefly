## Rollback and evidence

- Account failure: disable the account-access build flag and redeploy reviewed source; existing wallets and their historical records are unchanged. Preserve the provider account and its verified recovery methods. Test provider account deletion/recovery policies before further adoption.
- Media failure: close acceptance and keep public intake off. Leave moderation, current private-file authorization and owner withdrawal functioning. Preserve prior media/source records.
- Email failure: disable sending first, then enrollment; preserve unsubscribe, signed receipt and suppression behavior. Stop and reconcile ambiguous provider receipts, never repeat sends from a timeout alone.
- Export failure: rotate/revoke only the dedicated aggregate secret, leave stored receipts dated and show unknown on incomplete observations. Never copy production data into a local fixture.
- Pilot failure: pause the draft, pause matching links through the existing operator controls, communicate via the separately authorized support owner. No automatic external actions or funds.

The Launch Desk records operator-attested device/provider/payment test references for a specific release; a recorded pass is not independent verification and does not enable any flag. Do not label the product launch-ready until actual device, moderation, consent, operational and financial gates for the intended scope have been cleared.
