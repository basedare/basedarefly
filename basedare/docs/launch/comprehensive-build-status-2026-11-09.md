# Comprehensive build status — November 9, 2026

The shipped social P0/P1 core is not the entire master plan. This audit compares the locked unification brief, the IRL growth sprint and the harness addendum against current source and recorded acceptance. A feature being present does not establish real users, host permission, provider acceptance, a sale or a successful payment.

## Two different P2 labels

The original master prompt's **P2 is commercial validation**. The newer IRL sprint's **Phase 2 is instant local discovery**, while its **P2 is optimization**. They are not interchangeable. Core discovery already exists; commercial completion and optimization outcomes remain unproven.
