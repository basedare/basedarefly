# Sponsored performance rewards — design only

29 October 2026. No performance payout, fee, budget allocation or social-platform connection is activated.

Use fixed original deliverables as the first sale after normal authorization, scope, rights, funding and existing settlement readiness. Treat the new private sponsor brief as an operator proposal, not a public Campaign or payable obligation.

Before a future verified-view reward, define platform-authorized evidence access, creator account authorization, post eligibility/ownership and snapshot windows; deduplicate posts/accounts/campaigns; distinguish total/eligible/incremental views; exclude bought, repeated, fraudulent and out-of-window activity; cap per-post/creator/campaign liabilities before acceptance; version rates and currency; preserve snapshots, review and appeals; and support removals, privacy and evidence reversals. A URL, copied share or self-reported screenshot is insufficient. Do not scrape platforms or pay from uncapped counters.

Before qualified-action rewards, specify the exact independently supported action and its evidence. An attributable free start is not arrival, a QR+GPS presence claim is not a purchase, and a post review is not task acceptance. Retain existing durable authorization, anti-self-referral/collusion controls, dedupe, campaign budgets, funding reserves, signed commercial terms, review, settlement/retry/refund and dispute policy. No marketing intent may mark a paid task complete.

Reuse the existing Campaign/assignment/AttributionEvent/Dare/ContentRightsAcceptance systems if evidence justifies implementation. Preserve financial canon; any new pricing requires an explicit decision. Begin with a bounded manual reconciliation pilot after paid infrastructure is proven. AR/VR, new XP/tokens, auth migration, public bots and autonomous outreach remain outside this release.
