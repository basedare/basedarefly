# BaseDare investor memo

Updated 20 October 2026. Working discussion document; no investment terms or external submission are approved. Product and pricing authorities: [PHILOSOPHY](PHILOSOPHY.md) and [FINANCIAL_CANON](FINANCIAL_CANON.md).

## The promise

**BaseDare helps you find things worth doing nearby: try a free adventure, meet people, or earn from a paid dare. Share what happened and inspire the next person.**

The map connects real places to activities. Its commercial engine lets a buyer fund a specific action or deliverable with explicit evidence, reward and usage rules. BaseDare is an application on Base, not a separate blockchain.

Travellers and locals can find lists of places yet still struggle to decide what to do and who to do it with. Businesses need a clearer connection between a brief, completed work and evidence of the result. These are hypotheses to test through participation and buyer decisions; this memo does not establish market size, acquisition cost or willingness to pay.

## The initial loop

Discover a relevant activity → understand the commitment → join or try it → complete it → optionally share an invitation or result → another person participates.

Free participation does not require filming or a permanent creator role. Paid work has its own brief, funding, proof review and settlement. GPS or QR evidence of presence does not by itself establish task completion, a purchase or incremental business revenue. A buyer receives only the evidence and rights agreed for that scope.

## The first commercial test

Siargao is the founder-selected first market. Manila and Cebu follow only after useful supply, an accountable operator and repeat demand are demonstrated.

Reuse the existing **$2,500 Verified Field Sprint** when its scope fits: $2,000 managed service plus a separately tracked $500 gross contributor pool. Four accepted and settled $125 gross missions each pay $120 net; the $5 difference per mission is the settlement fee. A supported negative finding can be valid work. Do not sell a Field Sprint as guaranteed marketing reach or automatic commercial media reuse.

Self-service dares use **96% completer / 4% BaseDare / 0% referral**, with no Live Pot entitlement. Reward funding, grants and investment are separate from customer service sales. Subscriptions, a different business take rate and protocol licensing are future possibilities, not launched revenue streams.

The immediate target is one permissioned design-partner delivery with a real receipt and measured costs, followed by a full-price repeat or second sale. Any waived service fee is acquisition cost. Paid work starts only after the launch gates close.

## Why the network could compound

Repeated participation can improve activity supply, contributor reliability, reusable evidence templates and timestamped place history. Shared results can lead to another attempt. Those are potential advantages to prove, not an existing verification moat. Bespoke delivery without reusable assets or repeat demand risks becoming an agency business.

Measure unaided starts, completions, useful actions following shared links, voluntary returns, proof review/support cost, disputes, actual net payouts and buyer repeat decisions. Views, RSVP intent and self-reported attendance remain distinct from verified presence and paid completion.

## Funding purpose and ask

Capital would support payment/recovery reliability, a bounded Siargao experiment, measured verification/support and evidence of repeat demand. Amount, runway, instrument and valuation remain unset until the founder supplies cash, committed liabilities, costs and hiring assumptions.

The [$2m service-sales scenario](gtm/first-revenue-operating-plan-2026-10-19.md) requires 1,000 full-price Sprints and substantial fulfilment capacity. It is a stretch target, not a forecast or contracted pipeline.

Start with a product walkthrough and introductions to a suitable buyer, opt-in contributors and relevant early-stage investors. See the [verified funding shortlist and application brief](gtm/funding-shortlist-2026-10-20.md).
