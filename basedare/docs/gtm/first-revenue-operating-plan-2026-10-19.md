# First revenue, then repeatable growth

Updated 19 October 2026. Founder instruction: launch, onboard people and pursue millions in revenue within a year. Working planning target: **US$2 million of managed-service sales over the 12 months ending 18 October 2027**. This target and the ramp below are scenarios, not forecasts, contracted orders, approved spending or a guarantee. Currency and actual investment ask can be revised by the founder.

## Current work queue

1. **Close the money gate.** Configured Base mainnet escrow has no bytecode. Human signs the existing guarded V2 deployment; roles/token/fee checks, runtime cutover and a real payout/refund rehearsal follow. No work-around deposits, simulated receipts or promises of instant payout.
2. **Close the company gate.** Confirm legal operator/jurisdiction, monitored support, reviewer/backup and an explicit reward/gas budget. Prepare the numerical investment ask only after founder cash, burn and runway are known.
3. **Start actual conversations.** Six deduplicated prospects are in the vault; four exact email drafts are in the [first-batch queue](../../brain-vault/02-outreach/drafts/siargao-first-customer-batch-2026-10-19.md). Two need a proper contact route. This is prepared outbound, not a reply, meeting or signup. Confirm the sending account, review scope and check prior sent mail/opt-outs before sending.
4. **Get one customer decision.** Ask what outcome matters, who can authorize it, timing, budget owner and permitted rights. Show the existing product before adding features. A $2,500 Sprint is offered only for a fitting question; don't mis-sell it as guaranteed marketing reach.
5. **Recruit against the actual brief.** Ten proposed discovery testers, six screened contributors and one creator/manager remain the initial cell target. Contributors see exact funded work before any profile-building demand. Existing `/join`, `/earn`, `/brands/portal` and `/activations` remain the entry points.
6. **Reconcile one delivery and sell the repeat.** Log complete/failed/abandoned attempts, review cost, contributor net transfers, fees/refunds and a conservative buyer receipt. Keep creator amplification optional and separately permissioned.

## Advancement gates

| Stage | Evidence needed before expanding |
|---|---|
| First paid delivery | Deployed/verified escrow, actual payout and supported refund rehearsal, named operating owners, real device recovery, approved scope/rights, sufficient funded reward pool |
| Repeatable offer | At least one full-price buyer repeat or second sale, delivered receipt, actual costs and no unresolved identity/money-loss failure |
| More acquisition | Supported fulfilment capacity, contribution after review/support/acquisition costs measured, accepted evidence quality stable, no fabricated engagement or attendance claims |
| Another city | A responsible operator and useful activity supply; recovery and proof flow work without founder presence; city-specific budget and permission |
| Larger financing | Honest live demo, real customer evidence, named use of funds and a numerical runway plan. Grants fund experiments; they do not establish customer demand. |

## Daily operating record

One owner keeps a single prospect/contact history. At first, aim for two buyer conversations, three contributor/creator invitations and recorded replies per operating day only within the agreed sending scope. These are proposed human action targets, not an automated schedule. No automatic follow-ups or message bot has been enabled.

Count separately: sent messages with provider IDs; replies; booked meetings; qualified buyers; approved scopes; collected customer service cash; funded reward liabilities; accepted proofs; actual settlements; refunds; voluntary returns and full-price repeat decisions. Report current unknowns as unknown. The vault's zero recorded sends is not a claim that the production CRM contains zero leads.

The next useful commercial action is the first genuine conversation that identifies a buyer's decision and a willing participant. The million-dollar goal does not authorize spending, unreviewed rights, false readiness claims or scraping private contacts.
