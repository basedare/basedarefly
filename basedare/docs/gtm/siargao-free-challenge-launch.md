# Siargao free challenge: founder launch pack

## First release

`/adventures/siargao-verdict` — Is Siargao worth the hype?

Three stages: The hype → The underdog → The verdict. Free, self-guided, daylight, roughly 30–60 minutes plus travel. Start, resume, complete, save a card and invite a friend without signing in. No rewards, purchase, public upload or social post is required. Results are self-reported opinions, not verified attendance.

The feature runs from 28 October through 15 November 2026 in Philippine time. Discovery and new starts expire; authored routes and saved history remain. The map marker identifies the existing General Luna area, not exact Cloud 9 access or an event entrance. Direct links retain Siargao even when the visitor previously browsed a different city.

Recheck access locally before suggesting a physical meeting.

## Measure learning, not invented traction

Reuse existing analytics: landing, start, self-reported completion, explicit invitation share (native or copy), invitation open, then another adventure. Metrics require the configured analytics sink and real ingestion evidence. Landing events may repeat across reloads; they are not certified unique visitors. Native share completion does not prove a social post or downstream reach.

Do not repurpose paid-completion attribution as attendance or a reward. Existing tracked-link infrastructure needs a separate consenting-creator test before reporting creator-specific conversions. Start with a small manual log of handle, permission/contact status, volunteered feedback and next follow-up. No scraped contact list or automated send.

After the first observed attempts, fix the most common point of confusion. Ask whether they would send this to a friend without being asked. Only then add another challenge or commercial campaign.
