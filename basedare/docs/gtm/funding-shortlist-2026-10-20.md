## Reusable application answers

Drafts below do not imply every field in either form was inspected. Base's visible first step requests company name, explanation, website and X URL; step labels include Company, Team, Idea, Funding and Why Base.

**Product:** BaseDare helps people find things worth doing nearby: free adventures, local meetups and paid dares. Its map connects activities to places. Buyers can scope paid action or content with explicit evidence, reward and usage rules; participants see the work before investing in a profile.

**Stage:** Live consumer web product with a guest adventure path and existing creator/buyer entry points. Latest release passed application and contract CI. Production paid participation remains gated on human-signed escrow deployment, role checks, real payout/refund and operational readiness. No completed paid journey is claimed here.

**Why Base:** USDC settlement on Base is the intended paid-work rail. The application should make participation understandable to ordinary users without requiring them to understand the chain. Contract tests are not proof of production settlement.

**First experiment:** Siargao first. Prove unaided free participation and one permissioned, funded delivery; measure proof/support costs, then seek a full-price repeat or second buyer. The existing $2,500 Field Sprint separates $2,000 service from a $500 gross reward pool and is offered only for a fitting scope. Content promotion/reuse requires its own brief and rights.

**Evidence:** [Investor memo](../investor-memo.md), [release checks](../releases/first-customer-security-2026-10-19.md), live [Join](https://www.basedare.xyz/join) and [Adventures](https://www.basedare.xyz/adventures). Label demos as demos. Four outreach drafts are not customers. User/revenue totals and analytics are unverified, not invented zeros.

**Use of funds:** Payment/recovery reliability, bounded recruitment/delivery in one market, observed verification economics and repeat demand. Success means useful completions and repeat decisions, not incentivised post volume. No spend amount is approved by this brief.

## Founder facts required before submission

- Identity, relevant experience, actual team commitment, legal operator/jurisdiction and ownership.
- Verified contact email and X URL; no guessed handles.
- Prior funding, available cash, committed liabilities and monthly costs; runway and raise amount.
- Approved use of funds separating operating costs, reward liabilities, gas and contingency.
- Actual traction with source/window, or an explicit statement that it is not yet measured.
- Private confirmation of cap table and application history; review of exact completed answers and terms.

Once inputs exist, a planning equation is: **funding need = planned operating cash + approved reward/gas reserves + contingency + liabilities not already counted − available unrestricted cash − signed, collectible receipts**. Avoid double-counting costs; deposits/reward pools are separate from unrestricted operating cash. This is neither a valuation nor an accounting conclusion.

Complete the founder facts once, tailor and review both applications, then use their official routes. Meanwhile, [four prepared Siargao invitations](../../brain-vault/02-outreach/drafts/siargao-first-customer-batch-2026-10-19.md) can produce conversations once sender and review scope are confirmed. Grants/investment fund experiments; receipts and repeat decisions establish demand.
