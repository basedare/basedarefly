# Siargao first: funding, first users and creators

Prepared 19 October 2026. Founder confirmed Siargao as the first market; Manila and Cebu are later opportunities. This is an execution draft, not a funded offer, approved investment solicitation, booked campaign or partnership. No outreach has been sent. Reward budget, legal operator and launch owners remain unset.

## The promise and the first commercial test

**BaseDare helps you find things worth doing nearby: try an adventure, meet people, or earn from a paid dare.**

Start in General Luna with founder support. Free discovery is the entry point; a sponsor pays for a bounded, useful outcome. Do not charge travellers to use the map or turn optional Memories into the launch focus.

Keep three groups separate: travellers who want a worthwhile next move; contributors who answer a precise brief; creators/managers who may later share the experience. A place appearing on the map is not a partner. A contributor does not need followers, a public profile or a Baretag to request work alerts.

The first revenue offer reuses the existing **Verified Field Sprint**: $2,500 total = $2,000 managed service + $500 gross contributor pool; four $125 gross missions, each paying $120 net after successful 4% settlement. Expected delivery is 7–10 days after all prerequisites are met. This is pricing, not a sales forecast or margin claim. Track actual review, support, gas and acquisition costs separately. Any design-partner service-fee waiver needs an explicit recorded decision; never waive or underfund the reward liability.

For a content campaign, agree exact deliverables, publication and reviewed versioned rights first. The field Sprint grants no automatic advertising, resale or sponsor media-reuse licence. Do not promise multiple paid outputs from one winning bounty.

## Work in this order

1. **Close the payment and operating gates.** Use the [current readiness record](../releases/launch-funding-pilot-2026-10-19.md) and [human-signed cutover runbook](../runbooks/mainnet-cutover.md). Keep paid starts and payment collection closed until those gates pass.
2. **Qualify one buyer now.** Seek an observable question, a real decision, an authorized budget owner and acceptance of honest negative/inconclusive answers. Record a discussion or non-binding interest accurately; it is not revenue.
3. **Recruit a small Siargao cell.** Target 10 consenting adult discovery testers, six screened field contributors (four assigned, two reserves after funding), two permissioned distribution hosts, and one local creator or social manager. These are proposed targets, not recruited people. Groups may overlap, but the four independent answers need distinct contributors without disqualifying conflicts.
4. **Run the smallest paid rehearsal before the Sprint.** Proposal: two $5 gross tasks, one supported payout and one supported refund, plus gas. This $10 is not approved spending and must be counted separately from the pilot pool. Stop on first money/identity/evidence failure.
5. **Deliver and reconcile.** Freeze the actual question/place/window, publish only funded inventory, review evidence, save real receipts and get a buyer repeat/adjust/stop decision. Rejection alone is not proof of a refund.
6. **Amplify the receipt.** One creator can document a completed experience with explicit permissions. Sharing should lead to an exact activity or mission link. Measure useful participation and returns, not just views. Expand to Manila/Cebu only after this cell can operate reliably and the next city has an accountable local host and useful inventory.

## First brief to scope with a buyer

“By the agreed date, we need four independent daylight checks of whether selected public General Luna places are suitable for a solo visitor to sit for about 30 minutes, including visible price and entry conditions, because the result changes our guest recommendation sheet.”

This is a question draft. Select authorized places, dates, reviewer and budget with the buyer. No purchase, positive review, social post or identifiable bystander footage is required. Pay for a fresh supported answer, including a truthful closed/unavailable result. Keep the existing evidence, review, appeal and refund policies; do not promise unsupported replacements or instant payouts. The [earlier rehearsal brief](controlled-influencer-pilot-2026-10-14.md) provides the evidence/rights and owner checklist; its proposed $25 reward is separate and remains unapproved.

## Funding: customer cash and investment

**Customer/sponsor track:** Qualify the buyer and scope first; after readiness, collect the explicitly contracted service and reward lines using the existing managed workflow. Keep reward liabilities separate from operating income. Do not sell raw check-ins, promise attributable sales or collect informal deposits as a workaround for unavailable escrow.

**Investor track:** Use the live product demo and this honest status: “Discovery is deployed. The paid journey has local integration coverage, but mainnet escrow deployment and real payout/refund validation are pending. We are launching one founder-supported Siargao cell to measure useful participation and buyer repeat demand.” Do not describe tests, map entries, enquiries, proposed budgets or recruited intentions as paid traction.

An investment amount/valuation/instrument is not yet defined. Prepare the actual 90-day cash plan before setting an ask: verified payment/recovery operations; contributor liabilities; support and moderation; measurement; and locally accountable distribution. The founder supplies current cash, monthly burn, team costs and desired runway. Do not invent a raise size or a global growth forecast.

Investor update structure: problem → actual working demo → Siargao wedge → completed paid receipts and observed costs → buyer repeat decision → capital needed for the next measurable milestone. The four-percent settlement fee alone is a small revenue line at pilot volume; evaluate managed-service costs and repeat demand rather than calling gross reward volume revenue.

## Pilot scorecard and go/no-go

Keep denominators and failures: invited → consented → chose an activity → attempted it → returned; contributor screened → assigned → submitted → accepted → settled; buyer conversation → approved scope → funded work → receipt → explicit repeat decision. Separate organic discovery from paid participation.

Track actual gross pool, net contributor transfers, platform fees, refunds, service cash, service-fee exceptions, gas, reviewer/support time and acquisition spend. Report raw counts for this small sample; no statistically meaningful retention claim is implied.

Proceed to public promotion only after actual payout/refund receipts, physical iPhone/Android recovery tests, venue/rights permission, operating details, monitored support and no unresolved identity/money-loss issue. First success is one useful, honestly delivered and settled pilot with a buyer who makes a repeat decision. No paid ads or large influencer retainer before this evidence.
