## First brief, ready to adapt to an authorized place

**Title:** Help a solo visitor choose a café stop.

**Place:** One operator-selected public café in General Luna. Obtain venue permission for filming or interiors; no partnership claim is implied. Freeze its canonical place and coordinates before funding.

**Task:** During the agreed daylight window, report whether a solo visitor can currently buy a drink and sit for about 30 minutes. Record the displayed price of one ordinary non-alcoholic drink, visible seating and any stated entry/order restriction. An honestly supported closed/unavailable result is valid. No purchase is required or reimbursed by default.

**Evidence:** One fresh, permitted photo of public price/opening information and a short factual note, captured through the trusted proof flow at the place. Avoid identifiable bystanders, private conversations, restricted areas and copyrighted music. Do not manufacture a review or stage an arrival.

**Reward proposal:** $25 gross USDC to one approved contributor; successful settlement pays $24 net and $1 platform fee. This is a proposed operator-funded self-serve task, not an approved spend or funded inventory. No deposit, gas or reward transfer has been made.

**Window:** Set an explicit local date/time and deadline only after launch approval; do not launch an undated or expired brief. Approximate on-site effort: 15 minutes, excluding travel.

**Acceptance:** Evidence answers the locked question, matches place/time, is readable, and is owned/permitted. Negative answers can pass. Reject duplicates, fabricated observations, stale evidence and permission violations with a concrete reason. Review/appeal and any refund decision follow the actual supported product policy; do not promise instant payout/refund or invent a correction allowance that the interface cannot provide.

**Rights:** Contributor retains ownership. This field-evidence brief does not authorize sponsor advertising, sublicensing, resale or social-channel reuse. Review and receipt display follow the existing accepted product terms. A separate content/organic-social brief must identify the buyer, exact deliverable, publication obligation and reviewed versioned permissions before consent; keep `CONTENT_RIGHTS_APPROVED_VERSION` gated until that review is complete. Optional enthusiasm or being an influencer is not a licence.

**Publication:** None required on the contributor's social account. A manager may draft a case study after settlement, but creator/venue permission and a specific public-post approval come first. Disclose paid promotion where applicable; do not claim engagement or visits that were not measured.

2. Observe cold sign-in, existing Baretag resolution, wallet return and the exact mission continuation. Record friction without collecting passkeys, seed phrases or private keys.

No fabricated baseline, acquisition-cost claim or traction is implied.
